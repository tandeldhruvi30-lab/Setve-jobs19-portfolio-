About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur and inventor. He co-founded Apple Computer in 1976 with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne.

He helped introduce products such as the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone and iPad. His work influenced modern computing, mobile technology and product design.
